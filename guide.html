Why this exists

Sidekick Pro's name pickers are some of the most fun parts of youth group, but a list with every student's name on it doesn't work when half of them aren't in the room.

Our group is different every night. I didn't want to pick a student who wasn't there, and I didn't want to retype names before every game. We already check everyone in through Planning Center, so the list of who's here already exists.

Live Roster grabs that list, splits it however I need (guys, girls, high school, middle school, HS guys, MS girls, a single grade, or first-time guests), and gives me a Copy button for each. I paste it into Sidekick and we're playing. It's free to run, so I'm sharing it with any youth pastor who wants it.

📵Great for phones-away groups, too. If your group already puts phones away, or not every student has a phone, joining by scanning a QR code isn't really an option. Live Roster puts every student who checked in into Sidekick without anyone having to pull out a phone.

One-time setup

Set it up in about two minutes

You only do this once on each computer.

Open Live Roster

Go to liverostersidekickpal.github.io. Bookmark it, or install it with the optional step below.

Sign in with Planning Center

Click Sign in with Planning Center, log in like you normally do, and click Allow. There are no codes or keys to copy.

Live Roster can only see what you can see in Check-Ins, and it only reads names, gender, and grade.

Pick your youth events

Tick the Check-Ins events that are youth group (for example, MS Youth Group and HS Youth Group) and click Done. From then on, Live Roster switches to whichever of these events is checking people in tonight.

Put it in your Dock Optional

Chrome / Edge: click ⬇ Install app at the top of Live Roster, then sign in inside the app.
Safari: sign in first, then choose File → Add to Dock.

After that, it opens like any other app, in its own window.

Handy extras

Good to know

  • Your groups: Everyone, Guys, Girls, HS, MS, First-timers, HS Guys, HS Girls, MS Guys, MS Girls, plus each grade.
  • 30+ names? Sidekick's wheel holds 30, so Live Roster flags bigger groups. Use Pick Me for those.
  • Name style: choose first names, first name + last initial, or full names. Smart adds a last initial only when two students share a first name.
  • Paste format: one name per line, or commas, whichever works best where you're pasting.
  • Someone left early? Click 👀 on a group and untick them.
  • Missing gender or grade? A note tells you who's missing info, and you can set it right there. Your fix is remembered on that computer.
  • Different grade levels? In ⚙ Settings, you can change which grades count as middle school and high school.
  • Leaders who check in as volunteers are left out unless you turn them on in Settings.
Questions

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, and there's no account to make beyond the Planning Center login you already have.

Why Pick Me instead of the wheel?

Both work great! Sidekick's wheel holds up to 30 names, so on bigger nights, Pick Me is the way to go: it takes any size list, and its Bulk Add lets you paste everyone at once. The wheel is perfect for smaller groups like HS Guys or MS Girls.

Do I need to be a Planning Center admin?

No. You just need to be able to see your youth events' check-ins in Planning Center Check-Ins. If you can't see an event there, Live Roster can't either. Ask your church's Planning Center admin for Check-Ins access.

How does it know who's a first-timer?

Planning Center Check-Ins marks a student's very first check-in. Live Roster simply shows those students in the First-timers group.

Is my students' information safe?

Live Roster only asks Planning Center for names, gender, and grade. It never downloads addresses, phone numbers, or medical notes. The list goes straight from Planning Center to your browser, and nothing is stored anywhere except your own computer.

Will I have to sign in every week?

No. You stay signed in on that computer. Planning Center only asks again if you don't use Live Roster for 90 days.

It says no one has checked in, or I don't see my events.

Make sure the right events are ticked in ⚙ Settings, and that the event has a session today in Check-Ins. Live Roster shows tonight as soon as Check-Ins opens the session, and the list fills in as students check in.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Open the link on each computer and sign in once on each.

How do I sign out or remove access?

In Live Roster, open ⚙ Settings and click Sign out of Planning Center. You can also remove "Live Roster" from Authorized Apps in your Planning Center API settings at any time.
